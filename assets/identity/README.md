## Restrictions

- Do not add NASA logos or imply NASA endorsement.
- Do not reuse film characters or copy a commercial visual identity.
- Preserve source and license notes for every future asset.
- Do not ship rasterized AI wordmarks as the final logo; use the reviewed SVG sources.
- Use the approved character sheets as references for every future Nadir or RIVO generation.

See `docs/design/identity-exploration.md` for the identity rationale and proposed system.
