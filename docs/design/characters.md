# Character bible — Dr. Nadir and RIVO

**Status:** Canonical identity v1

**Visual sources:** `assets/identity/characters/`

## Relationship

Dr. Nadir and RIVO are comic opposites.

| Dr. Nadir | RIVO |
|---|---|
| Improvises | Validates |
| Talks with his whole body | Communicates with small movements |
| Celebrates before checking | Checks before celebrating |
| Uses metaphors | Uses exact language |
| Represents emotion and curiosity | Represents clarity and system connections |

Neither character is the player's enemy. Their relationship should make learning feel safe.

## Dr. Nadir

### Role

Mission-systems inventor, owner of the Orbital Workshop, and comic story lead. His mistake is not that he lacks intelligence; it is that he optimizes exciting parts before checking the whole mission.

### Visual invariants

- Egyptian man, about 40.
- Warm brown skin and compact energetic build.
- Short salt-and-pepper curls with a soft zero-gravity lift.
- Thick expressive dark eyebrows.
- Long angular nose.
- Narrow moustache and a small separated chin patch.
- Navy utility jumpsuit, cream torso panels, orange asymmetrical shoulders, cyan piping.
- Diagonal modular tool harness, calculation visor, magnetic boots.
- No lab coat, tie, giant glasses, or borrowed costume silhouette.

### Acting

- Leads with chest and hands when confident.
- Compresses inward when calculations contradict him.
- Reads a result twice before admitting it is wrong.
- Uses one silent look toward RIVO before a vocal reaction.
- Recovers quickly and turns embarrassment into a new test.

### Expression set

Confident explanation, suppressed panic, exhausted disbelief, delighted discovery, wordless groan, careful concentration, quiet apology, earned relief.

### Voice

Warm adult Egyptian voice in English, Arabic, or both depending on the final script. Delivery is quick when excited and slower when processing a consequence. Casting must be original; do not imitate a known actor.

## RIVO

### Role

Resource Integration and Validation Orb. RIVO connects player decisions to understandable consequences and protects the scientific boundary between evidence, simplification, and fiction.

### Visual invariants

- Small hovering sphere.
- Warm cream composite shell and navy seams.
- Orange side sensor and bumpers.
- Cyan light channels and hover ring.
- Black curved display face.
- Two small retractable utility arms; no legs.
- One short dorsal sensor fin.

### Acting

- Tilts rather than gesturing broadly.
- Changes hover height by only a few centimeters for emphasis.
- Uses a flat deadpan face after Nadir's confident errors.
- Warning state is coral and always includes a shape or icon.
- Never displays anger at the player.

### Face states

Neutral attentive, curious analysis, dry deadpan, constraint warning, quiet pride, processing, uncertain source, connection found.

### Voice

Clear synthetic voice built from an original performance or licensed speech system. RIVO speaks in short sentences, uses no sarcasm toward the player, and never presents a guess as a fact.

## Recurring props

- **Magnetic wrench:** visual callback; it slowly migrates through scenes when not secured.
- **Sealed coffee bulb:** signals Nadir's sleep deprivation without becoming a food-based identity.
- **Loose magnetic boot strap:** appears only in the wake-up scene and is fixed before mission operations.
- **Orange wrong-turn node:** identifies the accidental transfer in maps and story art.

## Story opening

1. Nadir prepares an automated orbital-transfer test.
2. He tells RIVO to wake him if anything becomes scientifically interesting.
3. He falls asleep in the test capsule.
4. The workshop executes the wrong saved transfer profile.
5. Nadir wakes in an unexpected high elliptical Earth orbit.
6. RIVO asks the player to help redesign a feasible return mission.

This is fictional framing. The final game must document the simplified return model and cite any NASA-derived constraints.

## Character-use restrictions

- Do not reproduce an existing character, actor, costume, vocal performance, or scene.
- Do not use a real person's likeness without written consent suitable for submission.
- Do not generate new character art without using the approved character sheets as identity references.
- Do not redesign Nadir or RIVO inside individual UI tickets.
