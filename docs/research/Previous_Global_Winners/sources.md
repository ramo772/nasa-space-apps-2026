# Sources and Verification Notes

[`interactive-games.md`](interactive-games.md). Official NASA Space
Apps, NASA, and GLOBE sources are prioritized for award status and
project descriptions.

## 2. Corrections Made During Verification

The initial research notes supplied for this folder contained useful
leads, but several statements needed checking:

- **Zumorroda-X:** Confirmed. NASA's 2025 announcement identifies it
  as the Art & Technology Global Award winner from Egypt and describes
  its farmer journey and mini-games.

- **TerraTales:** Confirmed. NASA identifies it as the 2024 Best
  Storytelling Global Award winner from Cairo, Egypt.

- **LunarTech Ensemble:** Confirmed. NASA identifies it as the 2023
  Best Use of Science Global Award winner from Egypt and describes its
  immersive lunar-seismic-data game.

- **Eco-Metropolis:** Confirmed. NASA identifies Innovisionaries as
  the 2024 Most Inspirational Global Award winner and describes
  Eco-Metropolis as a city-building game.

- **Team Voyagers:** Confirmed. NASA identifies it as the 2023 Best
  Storytelling Global Award winner and describes its interactive
  web-based water-cycle game.

- **Spacebee:** Confirmed as the 2023 Best Use of Technology Global
  Award winner. NASA identifies its project as a website integrating
  Apollo moonquake data.

- **ECLIPSE: CELESTIAL SHADOWS attribution:** Corrected. NASA Science's
  official 2023 announcement attributes this interactive educational
  tool to **Space Quest Maidens — Donzelas da Missao Espacial**, the
  Most Inspirational Global Award winner, not Spacebee.

- **Space Quest Maidens:** Confirmed as the 2023 Most Inspirational
  Global Award winner from Brazil. Its project teaches children about
  the mechanics of eclipses through an interactive educational tool.

- **EcoRangers and EcoNet:** Confirmed as 2024 Global Finalists in
  GLOBE Protocol Games. The source identifies them as informative
  games but does not establish detailed mechanics.

- **Quasar:** Confirmed as a 2024 Honorable Mention, not a Global
  Finalist or Global Winner.

## 3. Details Deliberately Not Filled In

The following details were not added where the reviewed sources did not
establish them:

- Public playable demos for LunarTech Ensemble, Zumorroda-X,
  Eco-Metropolis, Team Voyagers, Spacebee, EcoRangers, EcoNet, and
  Quasar.
- Public source-code repositories for those projects.
- Detailed gameplay loops, level design, controls, or mechanics for
  EcoRangers, EcoNet, and Quasar.
- Technology stacks where the reviewed sources did not explicitly
  identify them.
- A claim that a project is specifically a **2D** game unless a source
  establishes that format.

A missing link or detail is not proof that it does not exist. It means
it was not verified in the sources reviewed for this document.

## 4. Award Terminology

- **Global Winner:** One of the ten projects selected for a Global
  Award.
- **Global Finalist:** A project selected for the Global Finalist
  stage, but not necessarily selected as a Global Winner.
- **Honorable Mention:** A separate recognition; it must not be
  relabeled as a Global Finalist or Global Winner.

For 2024, the official NASA Space Apps finalist page lists 40 Global
Finalists. The GLOBE announcement separately identifies EcoRangers and
EcoNet as Global Finalists and Quasar as an Honorable Mention.

## 5. Source Quality

Preferred evidence order:

1. NASA award announcement or official NASA Space Apps award page.
2. Official NASA Space Apps team/project profile.
3. Official project website or demonstration linked from the team
   profile.
4. Relevant official science/education organization announcement
   (for example, GLOBE).
5. Team-member posts or third-party coverage only as supporting
   context, not as the sole basis for an award claim when an official
   source is available.
