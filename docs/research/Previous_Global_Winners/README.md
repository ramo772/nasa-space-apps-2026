# Previous NASA Space Apps Global Winners and Interactive Games

This folder contains a curated research collection of NASA Space Apps projects from 2023–2025 that can serve as references for our team developing an interactive game for the **Space Mission Design Game** challenge.

The research focuses on interactive games and game-like experiences, storytelling, exploration, simulation, scientific interaction, and gameplay mechanics.

This is not a complete catalogue of every game submitted to NASA Space Apps. It is a selected collection of projects with useful lessons for our game.

## Files

- [interactive-games.md](interactive-games.md) — Case studies, award recognition, project formats, verified descriptions, public links, comparisons, and lessons for our game.
- [sources.md](sources.md) — Official award announcements, team and project pages, demos, and verification notes.
## Key Findings

1. **Make science affect gameplay.** LunarTech Ensemble used lunar seismic data in an immersive game. Zumorroda-X used mini-games to explore agricultural challenges, while Eco-Metropolis used city-building decisions to teach sustainability.

2. **Give the player a reason to care.** TerraTales and Team Voyagers used storytelling to communicate scientific subjects and make them more engaging.

3. **Make choices meaningful.** Eco-Metropolis is a useful reference for connecting player decisions with environmental and resource-management consequences.

4. **Interactive does not always mean a conventional video game.** The examples include mini-games, simulations, interactive web games, and educational tools. Each case study identifies the project's format instead of treating all interactive experiences as the same.

5. **Keep recognition labels accurate.** Global Winner, Global Finalist, and Honorable Mention are different recognition statuses and should not be used interchangeably.

## Research Standards

To keep the research reliable:

- Award statuses are based on official NASA Space Apps or NASA announcements where available.
- Project descriptions are summarized from official project profiles or award announcements where available.
- A technology stack is included only when the reviewed material identifies it. Otherwise, it is marked **Not verified**.
- A demo, video, or source-code repository is linked only when a public link was found and verified.
- Missing details are not guessed. They are marked **Not found in reviewed sources** or **Not verified**.
- This is a selected collection of projects with game-design relevance, not a ranking of all Space Apps winners.
- Global Winners, Global Finalists, and Honorable Mentions are identified separately.

## Important Attribution Correction

The initial research notes contained an incorrect attribution concerning the 2023 projects.

According to NASA's official 2023 Global Winners announcement:

- **Space Quest Maidens** received the Most Inspirational Award and developed *ECLIPSE: CELESTIAL SHADOWS*, an interactive educational tool that teaches children about the mechanics of eclipses.
- **Spacebee** received the Best Use of Technology Award for a website integrating lunar seismic data collected by Apollo mission instruments.

These are two separate teams and projects and should not be confused.

NASA's 2025 announcement confirms **Zumorroda-X** as the Art & Technology Global Award winner.

Further details and references are available in [sources.md](sources.md).
