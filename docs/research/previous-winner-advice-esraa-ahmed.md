## How her team worked

Esraa Ahmed, 12:22 AM, after quoting "فانتوا الموضوع كان ماشي ازاي و كده":

> احنا كنا مستخدمين Phaser.js عشان نعمل اللعبة بس انتوا براحتكم طبعًا، وحاولوا تكونوا مخططين إزاي اللعبة هتمشي بنفسكوا، عشان بحس الـ AI مش أحسن حاجة في الـ creativity. فاحنا قعدنا كتير نفكر اللعبة هتكون إزاي بالظبط لحد ما عرفنا الفكرة، وكنا بنشوف الـ AI واحدة بواحدة باللي احنا عايزينه وكدا.

Reading, kept in her order:

- Her team used Phaser.js to make the game.
- She said this team is free to do otherwise ("انتوا براحتكم طبعًا").
- She asked them to plan for themselves how the game will play.
- Her reason: she feels AI is not the best place for creativity.
- They spent a long time deciding exactly what the game would be, until the idea was clear.
- They then looked at AI one piece at a time, for the specific things they wanted.

Her visible reply does not say whether a repository of resources and research, plus an agent that builds the game, is a good process. She answered by describing planning first and using AI narrowly.

Phaser.js is what her team used. It is not a decision for this project. `docs/technical/architecture.md` on this branch still marks the tech stack as not yet chosen.
