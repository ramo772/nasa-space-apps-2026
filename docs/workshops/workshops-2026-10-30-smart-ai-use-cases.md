## Purpose

Document the key concepts and practical insights shared during the session about the evolution of Artificial Intelligence and how businesses can leverage AI technologies.

The session focuses on understanding how AI models work, the role of AI Agents, the importance of Context Windows, and the capabilities of Multimodal Models in modern business applications.

## Content

### 1. Understanding AI Evolution

- AI is not a single technology; it has evolved through multiple waves.
- The current wave is **Generative AI**, which enables models to generate content and provide intelligent responses.
- Understanding these different waves helps identify how AI can be applied to real-world business challenges.

### 2. AI Parameters

- Parameters are fundamental components of AI models.
- They represent patterns and concepts learned by the model during training.
- The speaker explained this concept using an analogy of teaching a child that an apple is sweet through repetition.
- As the model learns from data, its parameters are adjusted to improve its ability to recognize patterns and generate outputs.

### 3. Understanding How AI Models Work

- Large AI models such as ChatGPT, Gemini, and Llama are highly complex.
- Although we understand their inputs (Prompts) and outputs (Results), the speaker highlighted that no one fully understands every internal mechanism of these large models.
- This demonstrates the complexity of modern AI systems.

### 4. AI Agents and Job Descriptions

The speaker introduced an interesting analogy between humans, employees, and AI Agents.

- An employee has a defined Job Description that specifies responsibilities and tasks.
- An AI Agent also needs a clear Job Description through well-defined instructions.
- Instructions alone are not enough; an AI Agent needs access to appropriate tools to perform tasks.

**Essential components of an AI Agent:**

1. **Job Description:** Clear instructions defining the agent's role and responsibilities.
2. **Tools:** Resources and integrations that allow the agent to perform actions, such as accessing calendars or emails.

The combination of clear instructions and appropriate tools makes AI Agents more capable of executing real-world tasks.

### 5. Context Window

The Context Window was described as the "bag" an AI model carries while processing information.

It includes:

- The initial Prompt.
- Uploaded files and documents.
- Conversation history.
- Instructions and additional context.

**Important considerations:**

- Every model has a limited Context Window.
- When the available context becomes full, older information may no longer remain available to the model.
- A larger Context Window allows the model to process more information, but it does not necessarily make the model more intelligent.

### 6. Evolution of Context Window Capacity

The speaker discussed the evolution of Context Window sizes across different AI models.

The examples mentioned during the session included:

| Model / Generation | Context Window |
|---|---:|
| GPT-3 | 2,000 words |
| GPT-3.5 | Approximately 4,000 words |
| GPT-4 | 32,000 tokens |
| Later generations | 100,000 tokens |
| Later generations | 200,000 tokens |
| Gemini 1.5 Flash | Up to 2 million tokens |

The speaker also mentioned that 2 million tokens represent approximately 1.5 million words.

*Note: These figures are reproduced from the session notes and are not independently verified model specifications.*

### 7. Multimodal Models

- Multimodal Models can process different types of data within the same model.
- These data types include text, images, and audio.
- Single-modality models focus on processing one type of data.
- Multimodal capabilities allow AI applications to understand and work with more complex inputs.

### 8. Practical AI Use Cases

The increase in Context Window capacity has enabled more advanced AI applications.

**Research Assistance:**

- Large documents and files can be provided to AI models.
- Models can analyze extensive amounts of information.
- This makes research and information analysis more efficient.

**AI Agents:**

- Agents can perform tasks using connected tools.
- Examples include accessing calendars and emails.
- Their effectiveness depends on clear instructions and suitable tool access.

**Multimodal Applications:**

- Models can process multiple data types.
- This enables applications that combine text, images, and audio.

## Key Takeaways

1. Understand that AI has evolved through multiple waves, with Generative AI representing the current wave discussed in the session.
2. Parameters are fundamental to how AI models learn patterns and concepts.
3. AI Agents require a clearly defined Job Description and appropriate Tools to execute tasks effectively.
4. Understand Context Window limitations when working with AI models.
5. A larger Context Window means the model can handle more information, not necessarily that it is more intelligent.
6. Multimodal Models provide the ability to process different types of data, including text, images, and audio.
7. Increased Context Window capacity makes use cases such as Research Assistance more effective.
8. Businesses can leverage AI technologies to support digital transformation and improve operational efficiency.
