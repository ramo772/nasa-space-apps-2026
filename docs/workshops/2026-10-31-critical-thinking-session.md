## Content

### How to choose the right challenge
- A good way to identify a problem is to look for something you have **personal experience** with.

### Design thinking steps
1. **Empathize:** identify a group of people who have a real problem, and understand their pain points.
2. **Define:**
   1. Research all existing alternative solutions, both direct and indirect.
   2. Make a comparison table between your solution and the other competitors.
   3. Find the key stakeholders.
   4. Discover the gaps in the market (or in previous solutions).
3. **Ideate:** use different techniques to generate a wide range of creative solutions.

### Ideation techniques named in the notes
- SCAMPER technique
- Six Thinking Hats technique

The notes list only the names; no steps or examples were recorded.

### Criteria for choosing a strong idea
A strong idea should have the following:
1. **Impact:** creates a real impact, even if it is small.
2. **Use of NASA data:** uses actual NASA data, not just a theoretical concept.
3. **Creativity:** it is not conventional and offers a unique perspective.
4. **Storytelling:** has a compelling story that can be communicated during the pitch.
