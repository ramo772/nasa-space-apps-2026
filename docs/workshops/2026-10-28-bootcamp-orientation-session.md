## Summary
This is a bootcamp orientation session for the NASA Space Apps Challenge, specifically for the Cairo local event. The speaker, Marwa, explains the structure of the hackathon journey, from the global challenge to the local Cairo hackathon and the upcoming bootcamp, emphasizing that participants don't need a technical background and that diverse skills are welcome.

## Key points
- The NASA Space Apps Challenge is a global hackathon where participants work on challenges using NASA's public data to create solutions within 48 hours.
- The Cairo local event is organized by IEEE Young Professionals and has been running since 2015, with global winners from Cairo teams in 2024 (Thera Tales) and 2025 (Zomoroda).
- The bootcamp will take place at Nile University and requires separate registration—registering for the global event does not register you for the local Cairo event.
- The Cairo hackathon dates are September 13-14, and the bootcamp is on Friday (September 6).
- Teams should combine different backgrounds (research, data analysis, technology, design, storytelling, art) rather than having all members with the same skills.
- The bootcamp includes sessions on understanding the journey and workshops on specific technical topics; some workshops may require a laptop.
- Participants should read the challenge briefs before the bootcamp and prepare five questions to analyze whether they can create a solution for a given challenge.
- AI tools are allowed in the hackathon, but participants must disclose which tools they used in their solution.
- Team formation is a key activity during the bootcamp, and it's okay to attend without a team or idea.

## Takeaways
- Register for the bootcamp using the QR code before the deadline, and also register separately for the Cairo hackathon when the link is announced.
- Read the challenge briefs on the global site and identify which challenges interest you before attending the bootcamp.
- Come with a growth mindset—the main goal is learning, not winning, and you don't need a technical background to participate.
- Follow the social media platforms for announcements about the agenda, venue details, and any errors or updates.
