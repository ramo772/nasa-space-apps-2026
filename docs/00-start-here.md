## Authority and precedence
1. NASA rules/challenge and authoritative dataset evidence for external claims.
2. Current explicitly approved product/architecture decision (supersedes old ones).
3. Linked approved issue for specific scope and acceptance criteria.
4. Meetings are historical context: **draft discussion is not a product decision**.
5. Unclear conflicts go to TL for decision; record resolution rather than guessing.
