## Video & Content Creation Tools

Tools used for downloading, compressing, summarizing, and editing video content.

| Name | Link | What it is for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| YouTube Video Downloader | [Loader.to](https://en.loader.to/4/) | Downloading videos from YouTube |
| Facebook Video Downloader | [FDown](https://fdown.net/index.php) | Downloading videos from Facebook |
| Online Video Compressor | [VideoCompress](https://videocompress.free/) | Compressing video files online to reduce their size |
| Video Summarizer | [URL to Text](https://urltotext.com/video-summarizer/) | Summarizing video content and extracting key information |

| Video Editor | [Microsoft Clipchamp](https://clipchamp.com/) | Editing videos, trimming clips, and combining video content |
