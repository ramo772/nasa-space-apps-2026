# The Odyssey of a Metaroid — Pitch Videos

This folder contains the pitch videos for "The Odyssey of a Metaroid," organized by sound option.

## Contents

- **Sound 1/** — Videos using Sound 1 audio
  - `Proposal_With_Intro_V1.mp4` — Same video as Sound 2, with intro, Sound 1 audio
  - `Proposal_Without_Intro_V1.mp4` — Same video as Sound 2, without intro, Sound 1 audio
- **Sound 2/** — Videos using Sound 2 audio
  - `Proposal_With_Intro_V2.mp4` — Same video as Sound 1, with intro, Sound 2 audio
  - `Proposal_Without_Intro_V2.mp4` — Same video as Sound 1, without intro, Sound 2 audio

## How to Choose

- **Sound 1 and Sound 2 contain the exact same video.** The only difference between the two folders is the audio track.
- **Each folder has two versions:** one *With Intro* and one *Without Intro*.
- The team needs to decide:
  1. Which sound to use: **Sound 1** or **Sound 2**.
  2. Whether to use the **intro** or not.

Pick one combination (e.g. Sound 2 + With Intro) and use that file for the final pitch.
